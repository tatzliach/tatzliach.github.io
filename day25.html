Transformation of Sentences 🔄
বাক্যের রূপ পরিবর্তন
"Let your conversation be always full of grace."
Colossians 4:6 🙏
Transformation of sentence মানে হলো **একই অর্থ রেখে বাক্যের রূপ পরিবর্তন করা**। এটি ইংরেজি বলার সময় খুব গুরুত্বপূর্ণ দক্ষতা। 📚
✅ Interrogative → Assertive ❓➡️✔️
  • Is he not your friend?
    He is your friend. 🤝
  • Can anyone deny this truth?
    No one can deny this truth. 📖
  • Is she not beautiful?
    She is beautiful. 🌸
  • Who does not love freedom?
    Everyone loves freedom. 🕊️
  • Did he not help you?
    He helped you. 👍
  • Can we forget our teachers?
    We cannot forget our teachers. 👩‍🏫
  • Is honesty not the best policy?
    Honesty is the best policy. 💡
  • Who does not like success?
    Everyone likes success. 🏆
  • Is this not a great idea?
    This is a great idea. ✨
  • Can anyone solve this?
    No one can solve this. 🧠
✅ Exclamatory → Affirmative 😲➡️✔️
  • What a beautiful flower!
    The flower is very beautiful. 🌸
  • How kind she is!
    She is very kind. ❤️
  • What a bright day!
    The day is very bright. ☀️
  • How brave the soldier is!
    The soldier is very brave. 🪖
  • What a wonderful idea!
    The idea is wonderful. 💡
  • How sweet the child is!
    The child is very sweet. 👶
  • What a great victory!
    The victory is great. 🏆
  • How beautiful the sky is!
    The sky is very beautiful. 🌤️
  • What a strong man!
    The man is very strong. 💪
  • How lovely the garden is!
    The garden is very lovely. 🌷
✅ Imperative → Interrogative 📢➡️❓
  • Close the door.
    Will you close the door? 🚪
  • Open the window.
    Will you open the window? 🌬️
  • Help me.
    Will you help me? 🤝
  • Bring the book.
    Will you bring the book? 📖
  • Call him.
    Will you call him? 📞
  • Finish the work.
    Will you finish the work? 💼
  • Wait here.
    Will you wait here? ⏳
  • Read the lesson.
    Will you read the lesson? 📚
  • Write your name.
    Will you write your name? ✍️
  • Bring some water.
    Will you bring some water? 💧
✅ Positive → Comparative 📊
  • No other boy is as tall as Tom.
    Tom is taller than any other boy. 🧍
  • No other city is as big as Tokyo.
    Tokyo is bigger than any other city. 🏙️
  • No other metal is as heavy as gold.
    Gold is heavier than any other metal. 🪙
  • No other river is as long as the Nile.
    The Nile is longer than any other river. 🌊
  • No other animal is as tall as the giraffe.
    The giraffe is taller than any other animal. 🦒
  • No other building is as high as this.
    This building is higher than any other. 🏢
  • No other player is as strong as him.
    He is stronger than any other player. ⚽
  • No other road is as long as this.
    This road is longer than any other road. 🛣️
  • No other mountain is as high as Everest.
    Everest is higher than any other mountain. 🏔️
  • No other fruit is as sweet as mango.
    Mango is sweeter than any other fruit. 🥭
✅ Positive → Superlative 🥇
  • No other boy is as tall as Tom.
    Tom is the tallest boy. 🧍
  • No other city is as big as Tokyo.
    Tokyo is the biggest city. 🏙️
  • No other river is as long as the Nile.
    The Nile is the longest river. 🌊
  • No other animal is as tall as the giraffe.
    The giraffe is the tallest animal. 🦒
  • No other building is as high as this.
    This is the highest building. 🏢
  • No other metal is as heavy as gold.
    Gold is the heaviest metal. 🪙
  • No other mountain is as high as Everest.
    Everest is the highest mountain. 🏔️
  • No other player is as strong as him.
    He is the strongest player. ⚽
  • No other fruit is as sweet as mango.
    Mango is the sweetest fruit. 🥭
  • No other day is as special as today.
    Today is the most special day. ✨
✅ Negative → Affirmative ➖➡️✔️
  • I do not hate him.
    I love him. ❤️
  • He is not poor.
    He is rich. 💰
  • She is not unhappy.
    She is happy. 😊
  • The task is not difficult.
    The task is easy. 👍
  • The day is not dark.
    The day is bright. ☀️
  • This is not useless.
    This is useful. 📚
  • He is not weak.
    He is strong. 💪
  • The answer is not wrong.
    The answer is correct. ✔️
  • The road is not narrow.
    The road is wide. 🛣️
  • She is not sad.
    She is happy. 😊

To Remember 📌

স্মরণীয়

  • Transformation মানে **একই অর্থ রেখে বাক্যের রূপ পরিবর্তন করা** 🔄
  • Interrogative ↔ Assertive বাক্য পরিবর্তন করা যায় ❓
  • Exclamatory বাক্যকে Affirmative করা যায় 😲
  • Positive → Comparative → Superlative রূপান্তর করা যায় 📊
  • Negative বাক্যকে Affirmative রূপে প্রকাশ করা যায় ✔️

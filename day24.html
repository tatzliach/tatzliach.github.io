Active Voice & Passive Voice 🔄
Active ও Passive বাক্যের ব্যবহার
"Whatever you do, work at it with all your heart."
Colossians 3:23 🙏
Active Voice এ **subject নিজে কাজটি করে**। Passive Voice এ **subject কাজটি গ্রহণ করে**। এই দুটি রূপ জানলে ইংরেজি বাক্য আরও স্পষ্ট ও শক্তিশালী হয়। 📘
✅ Active Voice ✍️
  • She writes a letter ✉️
    She writes a letter every day.
  • He reads a book 📖
    He reads a book before sleeping.
  • They play football ⚽
    They play football in the evening.
  • The teacher explains the lesson 👩‍🏫
    The teacher explains the lesson clearly.
  • I finish my homework 📚
    I finish my homework at night.
  • She cooks dinner 🍲
    She cooks dinner for her family.
  • We clean the room 🧹
    We clean the room every morning.
  • He drives the car 🚗
    He drives the car carefully.
  • The boy kicks the ball ⚽
    The boy kicks the ball strongly.
  • They build a house 🏠
    They build a house near the river.
✅ Passive Voice 🔄
  • A letter is written by her ✉️
    A letter is written by her every day.
  • A book is read by him 📖
    A book is read by him before sleeping.
  • Football is played by them ⚽
    Football is played by them in the evening.
  • The lesson is explained by the teacher 👩‍🏫
    The lesson is explained by the teacher clearly.
  • Homework is finished by me 📚
    Homework is finished by me at night.
  • Dinner is cooked by her 🍲
    Dinner is cooked by her for the family.
  • The room is cleaned by us 🧹
    The room is cleaned by us every morning.
  • The car is driven by him 🚗
    The car is driven by him carefully.
  • The ball is kicked by the boy ⚽
    The ball is kicked by the boy strongly.
  • A house is built by them 🏠
    A house is built by them near the river.

To Remember 📌

স্মরণীয়

  • Active Voice এ **Subject কাজটি করে** ✍️
  • Passive Voice এ **Subject কাজটি গ্রহণ করে** 🔄
  • Active → Subject + Verb + Object
  • Passive → Object + be verb + Past participle + by + Subject
  • Passive Voice সাধারণত ব্যবহার হয় যখন **কাজটি গুরুত্বপূর্ণ কিন্তু কর্তা গুরুত্বপূর্ণ নয়** ✅

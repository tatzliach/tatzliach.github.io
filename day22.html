All Prepositions 📌
Place, Time, Direction, and Common Usage
"Let your conversation be always full of grace, seasoned with salt."
Colossians 4:6 🙏
Prepositions হলো এমন শব্দ যা noun/pronoun এর সাথে বসে **স্থান, সময়, দিক বা সম্পর্ক** নির্দেশ করে। সঠিক ব্যবহারে বাক্য আরও **স্বাভাবিক ও প্রাঞ্জল** হয়। 📝
✅ Place Prepositions 📍
  • On 🪑
    The book is on the table.
  • At 🏢
    She is at the office.
  • Into 🚪
    He went into the room.
  • In 📦
    The toys are in the box.
  • Of 📖
    The cover of the book is red.
  • To 🏠
    I am going to my house.
  • By 🚶
    He stood by the window.
  • With 👩‍🏫
    She came with her friend.
  • Beside 🪑
    The chair is beside the table.
  • Between ↔️
    The shop is between the bank and the post office.
✅ Time Prepositions ⏰
  • At 🕰️
    The meeting is at 5 PM.
  • On 📅
    My birthday is on Monday.
  • In 🌞
    I was born in June.
  • Since 📆
    She has lived here since 2010.
  • By 🕒
    Finish the work by tomorrow.
  • During ⏳
    He slept during the lecture.
  • For 📖
    I studied for two hours.
  • Until 🌇
    We will wait until evening.
  • From 🏞️
    She stayed from morning to night.
  • To 🏡
    He works from 9 AM to 5 PM.
✅ Direction & Other Prepositions ➡️
  • Over 🌉
    The bridge goes over the river.
  • Among 🌳
    She stood among her friends.
  • Along 🛣️
    We walked along the road.
  • Across 🌊
    He ran across the street.
  • Behind ⬅️
    The garden is behind the house.
  • Under ⬇️
    The cat is under the chair.
  • Above ⬆️
    The picture hangs above the sofa.
  • Between ↔️
    The ball is between the shoes.
  • Beside 🪑
    He sat beside his friend.
  • Without 🚫
    She went out without her bag.

To Remember 📌

স্মরণীয়

  • Prepositions শব্দটি **noun/pronoun এর সাথে সম্পর্ক, স্থান, সময় বা দিক নির্দেশ করে** 📌
  • Place Prepositions → on, at, into, in, beside, between, among, over, by, with 🏠
  • Time Prepositions → at, on, in, since, by, during, for, until, from, to ⏰
  • Direction & Other → over, along, across, behind, under, above, without ➡️
  • সঠিক ব্যবহার করলে বাক্য **স্বাভাবিক এবং প্রাঞ্জল** হয় ✅

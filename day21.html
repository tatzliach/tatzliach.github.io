Temporals ⏰
Time Expressions for Past, Present, Future, Frequency, and Duration
"Teach us to number our days, that we may gain a heart of wisdom."
Psalm 90:12 🙏
Temporals হলো এমন শব্দ বা phrases যা সময় নির্দেশ করে। এগুলো **কথোপকথনকে স্পষ্ট এবং স্বাভাবিক** করে তোলে। 🕒
✅ Basic Time 🌞
  • Now 🕰️
    I am busy now.
  • Today 📅
    We will meet today.
  • Tomorrow 🌄
    She will come tomorrow.
  • Yesterday ⏳
    I saw him yesterday.
  • Tonight 🌙
    We will watch a movie tonight.
  • This morning ☀️
    I met her this morning.
  • This afternoon 🌤️
    We will study this afternoon.
  • This evening 🌇
    I will call you this evening.
  • Last night 🌙
    It rained last night.
  • Next week 📆
    We will travel next week.
✅ Frequency 🔁
  • Always 🔥
    He always wakes up early.
  • Usually 👍
    I usually drink coffee in the morning.
  • Often ⏰
    We often go to the park.
  • Sometimes 🌤️
    She sometimes helps me.
  • Rarely ❄️
    He rarely eats fast food.
  • Never 🚫
    I never lie.
  • Every day 📅
    I read every day.
  • Once a week 🗓️
    We meet once a week.
  • Twice a month 📝
    She visits twice a month.
  • Frequently 🔄
    He frequently travels abroad.
✅ Duration ⌛
  • For 2 hours ⏱️
    I studied for 2 hours.
  • Since morning 🌅
    He has been working since morning.
  • During the day 🌞
    I slept during the day.
  • All night 🌙
    They talked all night.
  • All day 🌞
    I was busy all day.
  • From morning till night 🌄
    She worked from morning till night.
  • For a week 🗓️
    We stayed in London for a week.
  • Since last year 📆
    I have lived here since last year.
  • During the meeting 🏢
    He stayed quiet during the meeting.
  • Until evening 🌇
    I will wait until evening.

To Remember 📌

স্মরণীয়

  • Temporals নির্দেশ করে **কখন বা কতক্ষণ কোনো কাজ হয়েছে/হচ্ছে/হবে** 🕒
  • Basic temporals → Now, Today, Tomorrow, Yesterday, Tonight 🌞
  • Frequency temporals → Always, Usually, Sometimes, Never 🔁
  • Duration temporals → For, Since, During, All day/night ⌛
  • Proper ব্যবহার করলে **বাক্য স্পষ্ট ও স্বাভাবিক হয়** ✅

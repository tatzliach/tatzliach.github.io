To Remember 📌

স্মরণীয়

  • Silent letters are letters in words that **are not pronounced** but affect spelling 📝
  • Common silent letters include **k, w, b, c, g, h, l, n, p, s, t, u** ✅
  • Silent letters often occur at the **beginning** (e.g., “knee” 🦵, “write” ✍️) or **within words** (e.g., “island” 🏝️, “castle” 🏰)
  • Knowing silent letters improves **pronunciation and spelling** ✨
  • Silent letters also help learners **recognize word families** and patterns in English 📚
  • **Practice reading aloud** to remember which letters are silent 👂
  • Some silent letters indicate **historical pronunciation** or **foreign origin** of words 🌍
  • Silent letters can change **meaning if mispronounced** (e.g., “knight” 🛡️ vs “night” 🌙)
  • Learning them builds **confidence in speaking** and writing English correctly 💪
  • Remember: **spelling ≠ pronunciation**; always pay attention to silent letters! 🧠
